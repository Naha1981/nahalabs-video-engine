# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## NahaLabs Video Engine — Production Commercial SaaS

### 1. Vision & Strategic Moat
NahaLabs Video Engine combines the upstream agentic orchestration capabilities of **OpenMontage** (pipelines, tools, scene plan decomposition, 35mm optical realism prompts, pre-compose validation, post-render self-review) with **NahaLabs Commercial Intelligence** (business understanding, brand brain voice matrix, 12+ industry vertical playbooks, multi-tenancy, approvals, usage metering, and closed-loop ROI attribution).

### 2. Primary Capabilities
1. **Interactive Timeline Studio:** Multi-track canvas player with live Ken Burns motion, real-time voiceover audio, animated karaoke captions, and multi-format aspect ratio switcher (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).
2. **Brand Brain Intelligence:** Enforces exact voice tone, WPM pacing targets, hex color tokens, typography, and strict compliance blacklists.
3. **12+ Vertical Industry Playbooks:** High-converting pattern-interrupt hooks, retention pacing curves, and visual metaphors across B2B SaaS, D2C E-commerce, Real Estate, Healthcare, Logistics/CargoIQ, Hospitality/Dining, FinTech, and more.
4. **Pre-Compose Validation Gate:** OpenMontage-inspired boundary validator enforcing speech velocity, scene count boundaries, and compliance blacklists before rendering.
5. **Post-Render Review Rubric:** Automated 6-point self-critique rubric scoring hook strength, visual realism, pacing, brand voice adherence, and CTA clarity.
6. **Cost Governance & Usage Metering:** Transparent per-second render and LLM token calculation with audited 98%+ agency cost savings proof.
7. **Client Approvals & Annotations:** Timestamped frame-accurate commenting, multi-role governance gates (G0-G3), and client share links.
8. **Universal MCP Gateway (`/api/mcp`):** Standardized JSON-RPC Model Context Protocol tools for Claude, Cursor, and ChatGPT.
9. **HMAC v2 Webhook Engine:** Fail-closed signed webhooks for Activepieces, Zapier, and Make syndication.
10. **System Health & Render Keep-Alive:** Real-time infrastructure monitoring, 10-minute ping schedulers, and 24-hour uptime graphs.
